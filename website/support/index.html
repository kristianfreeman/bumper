Support

Bumper is in beta. Thanks for trying it.

Getting started

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the Bumper beta link and install Bumper on your Apple TV, iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  3. Open Bumper and pick your Jellyfin server — it finds servers on your network — or type its address. Sign in, or use Quick Connect with a code.

Your other Apple TVs on the same iCloud account then sign themselves in.

Common questions

My iPhone can't find the Apple TV

  • Open Bumper on the Apple TV: it has to be open, not just installed.
  • Put the phone and the TV on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network).
  • On the TV: Bumper → Settings → iPhone Remote should be on.
  • On the phone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Bumper should be on.

Sound cuts out, or subtitles drift, away from home

Watching from a server over the internet, Bumper keeps a few seconds of the film ready ahead. If it still stutters, the film may need more than the server's upload speed can send: compare the file's bitrate (in Jellyfin's media info) with the server's upload speed.

The sound is late on AirPlay speakers or Bluetooth

AirPlay speakers hold back about two seconds of sound; Bumper keeps the picture in time with them. Turn on Settings → Speakers Button to pick speakers from inside the player.

A new film or show isn't showing up

Bumper updates when your server finishes adding it, and whenever you come back to the app. If it's still missing, check it appears in Jellyfin itself.

No subtitles came on

Settings → Subtitles → Show Subtitles decides when they turn on. If a file has none, the player's Audio & Subtitles → Find Subtitles searches for one that fits your exact file (your server needs a subtitles plugin, such as OpenSubtitles).

Tell us what happened

The quickest way is TestFlight: open the TestFlight app, choose Bumper, and tap Send Beta Feedback. It includes your device and version. You can also email support@playbumper.com or open an issue on GitHub.

It helps to say which device, where the sound goes (TV speakers, a receiver, AirPlay, Bluetooth), whether you're at home or away, and which film or show.
